# Mobile destination clarity: proposed next pass

Goal: visitors immediately understand the difference between opening an experience page and continuing the jungle journey.

1. Center only the active destination on mobile, keeping the jungle visible around it. Preserve environmental positioning on desktop.
2. Give each destination a distinct, visibly tappable primary action: EXPLORE THE MUSEUM, EXPLORE GLOW MINI GOLF, VIEW PARTIES & EVENTS, or SEE WHAT’S HAPPENING. Include a small supporting line: “Open the experience page.”
3. Keep NEXT: [DESTINATION] as a smaller secondary control below, with the existing single swipe cue. Do not add another overlay or navigation layer.
4. Let the active destination gently settle into view once; avoid popups, flashing, or repeated pulses. Respect reduced motion.
5. Validate at 320, 390, 430 and landscape widths. Check readability, safe areas, no overlaps, 44px minimum touch targets, and taps versus swipes.
6. Ask a first-time visitor to open the Museum page and then continue to Mini Golf without coaching. Confirm both actions are understood before publishing this layout change.

Status: proposal only. The current fix changes loading and motion timing; it does not redesign destination cards.
