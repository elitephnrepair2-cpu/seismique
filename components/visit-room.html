04 / BEFORE YOU GO

A little preparation.

Accessibility & sensory information +

Some exhibits include flashing lights. For wheelchair routes, accessible facilities, sensory needs, or other accommodations, speak with the Seismique team before booking.

Ask about accessibility ↗
What should I wear or bring? +

Wear comfortable shoes; high heels aren’t permitted. Bring socks for the indoor playground. Strollers, backpacks, and bulky equipment cannot go inside, and storage is not provided.

Can I take photos? +

Yes—photos are welcome. Leave flash and bulky photography equipment out of the exhibits.

Are food and drinks available? +

The Cosmique Café and Drinque Lounge offer refreshments. Outside food and drinks aren’t permitted, and food cannot be taken into the exhibits.

What’s included in my ticket? +

Museum admission includes the museum. Choose the Museum + Glow Mini Golf bundle for both experiences. Arcade play is separate. Contact Seismique for child-ticket eligibility or booking questions.

Policies & ticket conditions +

Review the ticket conditions at official checkout before purchasing. For refunds, re-entry or changes to a booking, contact the Seismique team.
