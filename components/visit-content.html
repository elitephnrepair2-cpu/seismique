Before you go

Do museum tickets include mini golf or arcade games?

Choose a Museum + Mini Golf bundle for both experiences. Arcade games are not included with admission.

What should I wear?

Wear comfortable shoes; high heels are not permitted. Bring socks for the indoor playground.

Can I take photos?

Photos are welcome. Flash and bulky photography equipment are not permitted.

Can I bring a stroller or backpack?

Strollers, backpacks and bulky equipment are not allowed inside. On-site storage is not provided.

Can I bring food or drinks?

General visits do not allow outside food or drinks, or food inside exhibits. Food arrangements for a private party should be confirmed with the event team.

Who qualifies for a child ticket?

Please contact Seismique to confirm the age cutoff before purchasing.

Where can I ask about refunds, re-entry or ticket changes?

Check the terms at official checkout or contact Seismique before purchasing.

Policies & ticket conditions
