A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

BEFORE YOU GATHER.

Can I book a private event online?

Private events are arranged directly with our events manager. Share your preferred date and plans so we can check space, staffing and supplies. Sending an inquiry does not reserve a date.

What if my group needs something different?

Tell us about it. The gallery accommodates up to 100 guests, with the possibility of extending into another room for approximately 150. The bar accommodates up to 65. Our events manager can discuss which arrangement suits your plans.

How do I send my inquiry?

Review your details and choose “Open email to send.” Your email app opens with the message prepared; you still need to send it. You can also copy the inquiry into your preferred email service.
