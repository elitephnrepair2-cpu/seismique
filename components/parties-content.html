A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

BEFORE YOU PLAN.

Does an inquiry reserve my date?

No. Share your preferred date and the events team can discuss availability and the steps to confirm your event.

Can I inquire before I know my budget?

Yes. Choose “I’d like help estimating a budget” and tell us your guest count and the kind of event you have in mind.

What if my event needs something different?

Choose “Other event” and describe your idea. Include any production, timing or space requirements so the team can discuss what’s possible.

How do I send my plan?

Review your details and choose “Email my event plan.” Your email app opens with the message prepared; you still need to send it. You can also copy the plan into your preferred email service.
